## Observable by a package: isolates and frozen values

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-ISOLATE | `isolate-spawn`, `isolate-create`, `isolate-send`, `isolate-receive`, `isolate-await`, `isolate-on-done`, `isolate-future-cancel`, `isolate-interrupt`, `isolate-kill`, `freeze`, `frozen-p`, `thaw`, `buffer-snapshot`, `with-snapshot-buffer`, `buffer-snapshot-current-p`, `isolate-retire`, `isolate-zygote`, `isolate-zygote-list` | Not in GNU. GNU's concurrency is `make-thread` (cooperative, one global lock); an uncaught error in a thread is stored in `thread-last-error` and `thread-join` returns normally. | Worker VMs are child processes that adopt the spawner's world (`--isolate`), run `(apply FUNCTION ARGS)` on frozen arguments and answer with a frozen result or the error, delivered at wait points (the command loop's idle, `isolate-await`). `isolate-await` re-signals the worker's error with its condition and transportable data, defining an unknown condition with the worker's `error-conditions` ancestry; unfreezable data elements arrive as `(isolate-unfreezable PRINTED)`. A worker's death resolves its futures with `isolate-died`, a kill with `isolate-killed`. The worker's backtrace is not transported (`isolate-error-backtrace` stays nil). `isolate-interrupt` quits CPU-bound Lisp at the engine's safepoints, honouring `inhibit-quit`; a job blocked in a primitive (a `call-process` wait) is not interrupted until it returns, and `isolate-kill` follows after `isolate-interrupt-timeout`. GNU's `make-thread` family is not implemented (void). `buffer-snapshot` freezes a buffer's accessible text with its start, ticks, point, file name, directory, mode, generation and chosen locals; `with-snapshot-buffer` gives a worker a buffer of its own over that text in that mode, with writable properties and read-only text (a change made under `inhibit-read-only` signals when the body returns). `isolate-retire` ends an idle worker with its spawner's bye, without waiting and without a death report. `isolate-zygote` starts (or describes) the zygote for a feature list, a worker prepared once that forks a child per pooled job; `isolate-zygote-list` describes them all. | A package observes these only by calling them. Re-signalling is deliberate: a background job's failure must reach the code that asked for it.
