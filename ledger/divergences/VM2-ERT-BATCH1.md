## GNU ERT batch execution

| # | Mechanism | GNU | Romacs | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-ERT-BATCH1 | Explicit batch adapter and compiled backtrace metadata | ERT isolates each test with a temporary buffer and a window excursion; GNU backtraces can describe interpreter/source frames and lexical locals. | The named `romacs-batch` adapter retains the temporary buffer, assertions, error handling, cleanup, expectedness and GNU batch runner, and replaces the precautionary window excursion. Condition objects use `prin1` plus a newline instead of editor indentation; their data and expectedness are unchanged. Automatic window-layout restoration and GUI fixtures are unsupported. Backtraces report real compiled calls, original evaluated argument snapshots and dynamic locals; lexical/source locals and pre-argument named-autoload frames are unavailable; `backtrace-eval` and `backtrace-debug` act on these frames as on GNU byte-code's (eval.c unrewind of bindings, current buffer and excursions; debug-on-exit flags). GNU apply optimizations can produce different frame/argument shapes. Ordinary closures print opaquely through an owned `cl-print-object` method. | Keep the batch regime explicit and preserve private closure representation. | <!-- symbols: ert--run-test-internal, mapbacktrace, backtrace--locals, cl-print-object -->
