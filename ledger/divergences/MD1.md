## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MD1 | Markdown modes | GNU ships `markdown-ts-mode` (tree-sitter-markdown, `lisp/textmodes/markdown-ts-mode.el`); jrblevin's `markdown-mode` is an ELPA package | Romacs ships neither. `romacs-markdown-mode` edits Markdown on Romacs's one mechanism (pulldown-cmark in `crates/romacs-vm/src/markdown`), and `.md`, `.markdown`, `.mdx`, `.mkd` and `.mdown` files open in it. `markdown-mode`, `gfm-mode`, `markdown-ts-mode` and `markdown-ts-mode-maybe` are aliases of it, `markdown-view-mode`, `gfm-view-mode` and `markdown-ts-view-mode` of `romacs-markdown-view-mode` (`lisp/textmodes/markdown-mode.el`, `lisp/textmodes/markdown-ts-mode.el`); `markdown-mode-hook` and `markdown-mode-map` are its hook and map. markdown-mode's and markdown-ts-mode's other functions do not exist, nor do the tree-sitter markdown grammars | tree-sitter-markdown agreed with the CommonMark and GFM examples on 89.6% and cost 1.1G instructions a key on a 1 MB file; `romacs-markdown-mode` costs about 1M there (GNU's markdown-mode 13.8M). One mechanism serves files, agent replies and documentation views. A package that calls markdown-mode's own functions installs markdown-mode from ELPA, whose files load ahead of the aliases | <!-- symbols: markdown-mode, gfm-mode, gfm-view-mode, markdown-ts-mode, markdown-view-mode, romacs-markdown-mode -->
