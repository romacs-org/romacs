| VM2-QUIT-POINTS | Where a pending quit is taken | `maybe_quit` runs at every `eval_sub` and `funcall` and, in byte code, at every call and every 256th backward branch (`bytecode.c`); a catch landing defers its check to the next backward branch (`bytecode.c:978-982`). | A quit is taken at an engine's polls: every 10,000 ticks (backward jumps, Lisp function entries, inline-body hits, continuation calls), at the next tick once something zeroed the budget (a handler's landing, a primitive's return when a poll is due), at an engine's entry, and in long primitives' own polls.
