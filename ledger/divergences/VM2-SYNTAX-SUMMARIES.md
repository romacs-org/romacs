## Observable by a package: syntax summaries

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-SYNTAX-SUMMARIES | A long `parse-partial-sexp`, and `syntax-ppss` | Every call scans each character from FROM to TO; `syntax-ppss` parses from states syntax.el caches, invalidated below each edit. | From FROM to TO over at least four leaves of a multibyte text, with no TARGETDEPTH, STOPBEFORE or COMMENTSTOP and, where `syntax-table` properties are honoured, propertized past TO, the scan reads only its two end leaves and composes kept summaries of the leaves between (`crates/romacs-vm/src/syntax_index.rs`, `syntax_summary.rs`); every value, point included, is the scanner's. Summaries are kept per syntax table, by a serial no other table takes (not its address), and per count of char-table writes, which every change of any char table's characters, default, parent or extras (`modify-syntax-entry`, `aset`, `set-char-table-range`, `set-char-table-parent`, `set-char-table-extra-slot`, `optimize-char-table`) advances, per `comment-end-can-be-escaped` and per `parse-sexp-lookup-properties`; a `syntax-table` property change drops those of the text it covers. A syntax descriptor changed in place (`setcar` on a `(CODE . MATCH)` cons a table or a `syntax-table` property holds) is not seen until some char table, or that property, changes: descriptor conses are shared values (`string-to-syntax` returns one cons for every plain descriptor, as GNU's `syntax_code_object` does), and no GNU or bundled code mutates one. `syntax-ppss` is syntax.el's; its long parses from the states it caches are the ones served. `romacs-inhibit-syntax-summaries` scans every character; `romacs-syntax-summaries-check` scans as well and signals where they differ; `romacs-syntax-summaries-stats` shows what was served. | An edit near the top of a large file no longer costs a scan of all the text after it. Tests: `tests/syntax_summaries.rs` (against pinned GNU, with edits), the seeded property tests in `syntax_scan.rs`, `syntax_summary.rs` and `syntax_index.rs`. | <!-- symbols: parse-partial-sexp, syntax-ppss, modify-syntax-entry -->
