## Font selection order

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-GLYPHLESS-NOFONT | A character no installed font has a glyph for (`glyphless-char-display`'s `no-font` slot) | GNU (`xdisp.c` produce_glyphless_glyph, `xterm.c` x_draw_glyphless_glyph_string_foreground) shows the slot's method in face `glyphless-char` merged over the character's: a one-pixel thin space, an empty box CHARACTER_WIDTH columns of the base font's average width, or a box of the code (`hex-code`, the default) or an acronym in two rows of that face's text; a box taller than the base font makes its row taller. A face whose font cannot be opened uses the frame's font. | Romacs shows the same methods at the same widths and offsets (`romacs-layout2` `glyphless`), the character bound at that width so measurement, motion and the cursor read what is painted. `glyphless-char` is its default rather than the face's current attributes (the character's face at 0.6 of its size, in its foreground), its base width is the advance of `x` in the character's font (GNU's `average_width`, equal in a monospace font), an empty box is one column, a box never makes its row taller (its text stays centred on the row), and in the tab and status bars, whose clicks find characters by their glyphs, the box is empty. A face whose font cannot be had falls back to the default face's family, then `monospace`; each such fallback is reported once on stderr. | Display never stops for a missing glyph; GNU's default face and method are what sessions see. Pinned by `crates/romacs-layout2/tests/glyphless.rs` and `crates/romacs-vm-gui/harness/glyphless_live.py`. |
