| VM2-IMAGE-DISPLAY | In-line images (`(image ...)` and `((slice ...) (image ...))` display specs) | xdisp.c's produce_image_glyph places an image glyph on the row's common baseline, as it does every glyph; the cursor on an image without a mask is a rectangle around it; a relief's corners are mitred and rounded (pgtk_draw_relief_rect); `:ascent center` centres on the font of the glyph's face. | The image glyph, its margins, relief and slice, measure and paint as GNU's (`harness/images_live.py` display: `line-pixel-height`, `window-text-pixel-size`, `posn-at-point`, `vertical-motion`). The host aligns a row on one baseline only when it holds an image (or a box); a text-only row of mixed faces keeps each glyph at the row's top and the tallest glyph's height, measured the same way. The cursor on an image is drawn under it. A relief's sides are square. `:ascent center` centres on the default face's font. An image whose decode fails is GNU's hollow rectangle. On a HiDPI output an image is decoded at the display's device scale and laid out in logical pixels, as GNU's pgtk does; a scale change that arrives while a prompt waits applies from the next one the command loop reads. A raster image, or an SVG whose root gives its size, decodes off the evaluator (images.md section 7, Stage 2b): it is laid out at once at the size its header gives, which its decode reports, and its slot shows the face's background until the pixels land and repaint; one that fails after a valid header is laid out again as the placeholder, its loader's message logged to *Messages*. `image-size`, `image-mask-p`, `image-metadata` and `lookup-image` wait for the decode as GNU's synchronous load does; `C-g` quits that wait. | The Lisp-visible geometry of image rows follows pinned GNU; mixed-face text rows keep the host's existing layout, which motion must agree with. | <!-- symbols: insert-image, put-image, create-image, posn-at-point -->
