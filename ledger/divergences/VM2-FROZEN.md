|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-FROZEN | Writes to frozen and executable-image objects | GNU raises `(error "Attempt to modify read-only object" OBJ)` for pure-space and constant strings (`CHECK_IMPURE`); it has no user-creatable read-only conses, vectors, records, bool-vectors, char-tables or hash tables. | `freeze` copies a value graph (conses, strings with their text properties, vectors, records, floats, bignums, bool-vectors, char-tables, non-weak hash tables, symbols by name) into an immutable region; every mutator of such an object (`setcar`, `setcdr`, `nreverse`, `nconc`, `sort`, `delq` past the head, `aset`, `fillarray`, `clear-string`, the text-property writers, `puthash`, `remhash`, `clrhash`, `set-char-table-range`, `set-char-table-parent`, `set-char-table-extra-slot`, `modify-category-entry`) signals GNU's read-only error with the object, as do writes to executable-image strings and conses (which signalled a VM2 text before). `copy-sequence` and `thaw` give private copies.
