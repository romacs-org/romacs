## VM2 format-message boundary

| # | Mechanism | GNU | Romacs | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-FORMAT-MESSAGE1 | General formatting and initial locale capability | `format-message` has all `format` conversions and text-property/identity behavior; the initial internal quoting flag follows the process locale. | Quote selection, template-only substitution, explicit capability bindings, display-table fallback and byte8 promotion are implemented. The shared formatter supports field numbers, flags, width and precision for `%s`, `%S`, `%c`, `%d`, `%i`, `%b`, `%B`, `%o`, `%x`, `%X`, `%e`, `%f`, `%g` and `%%`, including bignum integers and float rounding. Width, precision and field-number components are capped at 2^20. Text properties propagate as in `format` (VM2-FMT1), except on the path that promotes raw octets beside Unicode text, which returns no properties; GNU string-identity behavior remains unsupported. Initial capability is the VM UTF-8 default (`t`), without host non-UTF-8 locale detection, and the capability binding is not boolean-normalized. | `tests/format_numeric.rs` compares the formatting domain with pinned GNU; the separate 64-case quoting corpus does not establish text-property, identity or host-locale parity. | <!-- symbols: format, format-message, text-quoting-style -->
