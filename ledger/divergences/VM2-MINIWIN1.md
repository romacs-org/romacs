## Observable by a package: mini-window display

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-MINIWIN1 | Overlay strings and mini-window height | Redisplay shows every window's overlay `before-string` and `after-string` (xdisp.c load_overlay_strings) and resizes the mini-window to its display within `max-mini-window-height` as `resize-mini-windows` says (resize_mini_window; loadup.el sets `grow-only`). | The mini-window shows its buffer's overlay strings in load_overlay_strings' order, with their `face` properties (a face list merged as xfaces.c does) and a `cursor` property placing point, and redisplay sizes it as resize_mini_window does, including `redisplay-adhoc-scroll-in-resize-mini-windows`; the shared chain sets `grow-only` after window.el as loadup.el does. Other windows show no overlay strings yet; strings' own `display`, `invisible` and image properties are not interpreted; a tab in a string shows as spaces and other controls as `^X`; a line wider than a row counts the rows its window breaks it into, at words under `word-wrap` as GNU's wrap points do, in columns of the frame's character width between the window's fringes (its frame's, as GNU's mini-window has; a mini-window a layer hosts has the layer's, none), less, without a right fringe, the continuation glyph's advance (the display table's `wrap` glyph, else `\`; the host's advance where it lays the frame out, else its columns), a tab to its next stop from the line's start, and leaving the outermost minibuffer resizes the mini-window at once (minibuf.c read_minibuf_unwind). Rows are a uniform line high: a face whose font is taller (GNU's `help-key-binding` in `fixed-pitch`) does not make its row taller, as GNU's mixed-font rows are. | Completion UIs (icomplete, fido) display their candidates this way. Other windows' point visibility and scrolling count buffer lines (redisplay.rs), which overlay strings would invalidate, so they stay out until that counts display lines. `crates/romacs-vm/tests/mini_window.rs`; the GUI journey `crates/romacs-vm-gui/tests/gnu_completion.rs` and `harness/completion_live.py`. | <!-- symbols: resize-mini-windows, max-mini-window-height, redisplay-adhoc-scroll-in-resize-mini-windows, before-string, after-string -->
