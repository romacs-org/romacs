## VM2 explicit garbage collection

| # | Mechanism | GNU | Romacs | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-GC-GENERATIONS | Automatic collections of the young alone | Every collection marks and sweeps the whole heap (`alloc.c` `garbage_collect`), and each one runs `post-gc-hook`, counts in `gcs-done` and `gc-elapsed`, and cuts undo lists past `undo-limit`, `undo-strong-limit` and `undo-outer-limit` (`compact_buffer`, `truncate_undo_list`). | An automatic collection that only allocation since the last collection made due (4 MiB at a cons's charge, `MINOR_BYTES`) is a minor: it frees what was made since then and nothing reaches, every older entry counting as reached, and reads of the old only what may name the young (entries stored into, buffer text and char-table nodes written since, written symbol slots, functions changed, logged hash puts, call caches filled, names made). Like a full it runs finalizers of what it frees and `post-gc-hook`, and counts in `gcs-done` and `gc-elapsed`. A full comes when the old generation has grown by `gc-cons-threshold` or one traced heap (VM2-GC-PACING: what the minors promoted, objects and payload), at idle (VM2-GC-IDLE), for `garbage-collect`, for the request code compiled, and where a compaction is asked; a job's child and a compaction collect only in full. What only a full does: undo lists are offered and cut, old garbage, dead obarray symbols and frozen regions go, code is retired, weak entries of old keys are dropped. So garbage that dies young brings no undo cut where GNU would cut at each collection it makes. Under `ROMACS_GC_VERIFY` each minor is checked against a whole reachability walk (`Vm::check_minor`). `ROMACS_GC_MINOR=0` makes every collection a full. Tests: `gc::tests` (`a_minor_*`), `gc_check::tests`. | **Lisp-observable**: `gcs-done` grows by many short collections, `post-gc-hook` runs after each, and undo lists are cut later than GNU cuts them, at a full. | A full's pause grows with the live heap: a 54 MB heap took 100-400 ms at every collection, one a second while an agent board refreshed. A minor's grows with the young: p50 4.0 ms, p99 6.7 ms on that heap's shape at host load 52, and fulls became rare (one in 494 collections over an editing session's batch run). | <!-- symbols: garbage-collect gcs-done gc-elapsed post-gc-hook undo-outer-limit -->
