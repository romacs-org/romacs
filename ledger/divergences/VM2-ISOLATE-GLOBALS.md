## Observable by a package: isolates and frozen values

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-ISOLATE-GLOBALS | What a worker sees of the spawner | n/a (one heap). | A worker starts from the world's globals plus `isolate-inherited-variables` (thawed into worker-owned copies) and a job's `:bindings`; it never sees the spawner's later `setq`s, loaded packages or user init. Where pooled jobs run on forked workers (`isolate-zygotes`; a session by default), each job runs in a fresh child of a zygote prepared once with its `:require` set, in the spawner's `default-directory` unless it names one, and sees nothing an earlier job left; the zygote is retired once an inherited variable, a file it loaded or a `load-path` directory changes. Such a job runs no collection until it has grown the heap by `isolate-job-memory-headroom` bytes, and a collection that finds live growth past that ends its child at once (VM2-ISOLATE-MEMORY). With no room in the pool (`isolate-pool-size`, by default the processors of the zygote's forking thread minus one, or `isolate-pool-memory-limit`) a job waits, its future on no isolate until it is dispatched; a zygote past `isolate-zygote-memory-limit` is refused and its features run on pooled workers. Elsewhere a pooled worker keeps its own symbol values between jobs of the same `:require` set; a job that changed `features` retires its worker from the pool. | Racket's and Python's contract for isolated interpreters; design §3.5, §7; worker-vms note §2.3-2.4. | <!-- symbols: isolate-inherited-variables, isolate-zygotes, isolate-job-memory-headroom, isolate-pool-size, isolate-pool-memory-limit, isolate-zygote-memory-limit -->
