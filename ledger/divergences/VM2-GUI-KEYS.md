| VM2-GUI-KEYS | Ctrl+m, Ctrl+i and Ctrl+[ on the GUI | A GUI frame folds Ctrl with a letter or `@[\]^_` into its ASCII control character (keyboard.c `make_ctrl_char`), as a terminal must, so Ctrl+m is RET (13), Ctrl+i TAB (9) and Ctrl+[ ESC (27): one key each with Return, Tab and Escape once those are translated, and no keymap can tell them apart. `single-key-description` keeps a modifier prefix inside the brackets when one character follows it (`<C-m>`, `C-<M-m>`), which `key-valid-p` refuses, and `key-valid-p` takes no punctuation name in brackets | The GUI delivers them as the symbol events `C-m`, `C-i` and `C-[`, with any other modifiers in GNU's order (`C-M-m`, `C-S-i`; `crates/romacs-vm-gui/src/native.rs`); Return, Tab and Escape are the function keys `return`, `tab` and `escape`, as GNU's X and pgtk frames deliver them.
