| VM2-SESSION-IMAGE | Carrying a session into a new process (`romacs-restart`, `--restore IMAGE`) | desktop.el saves buffers that visit files or have a handler, by file name and position, and savehist the histories; unsaved text is lost, and a restart is two separate steps. | `warm-restart-write` writes a session image: one printed plist (buffers by `warm-restart-buffer-id` with point, mark, mark ring, narrowing, modes, `desktop-locals-to-save`, `hard` newlines and the undo list as data; the window state of each frame; the kill ring, registers, histories and `warm-restart-globals`) beside the raw internal text of every unsaved or non-file buffer, through `romacs--session-image-write` and `-read` (owner-only, synced, renamed into place, each part with a digest that a reader checks before anything is applied). `romacs-restart` writes one, runs `kill-emacs-hook`, starts the next process (this build, or `romacs-restart-command`) with `--restore IMAGE` and exits; the new process restores it after its init, puts the buffer list back in its order in one change (`romacs--lead-buffer-list`), and lays the windows out after the startup hooks. Buffers whose mode has a `desktop-buffer-mode-handlers` entry come back through it, under `warm-restart-passive`, and attach from `warm-restart-activate-functions`.
