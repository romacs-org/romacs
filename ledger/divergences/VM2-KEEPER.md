## Observable by a package: the keeper

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-KEEPER | What outlives the session process | Nothing: every descriptor and child ends with Emacs. | A session may attach to a keeper (`romacs-keeper-attach`), a small process of its own per session name that holds kept descriptors and is the parent of kept children. The session is handed duplicates and uses them directly. When the session process is restarted or dies, kept children block on a full pipe instead of dying and a successor claims their handles (`romacs-keeper-claim`); when it quits, its handles close unless marked kept. A kept child's `getppid` is the keeper, for good, and it runs in the keeper's cgroup and limits, with exactly the environment, directory, umask, signal mask and descriptors its spawn names. `emacs-pid` stays the session process's own pid; `romacs-session-pid` is the keeper's while one is attached. Batch sessions use no keeper unless they attach themselves. | A restart or a crash should not end a shell or an agent; `crates/romacs-keeper` (its rules are in the crate's docs), `test/romacs/lisp/romacs-keeper-tests.el`. | <!-- symbols: romacs-keeper-attach, romacs-keeper-claim, romacs-session-pid, romacs-keeper-handles, romacs-keeper-enabled, romacs-keeper-idle-seconds -->
