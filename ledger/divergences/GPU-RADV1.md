## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPU-RADV1 | `RADV_PERFTEST` in the environment | emacs.c `main` leaves the environment it was started with alone but for its own variables (`TERM`, `INSIDE_EMACS` in subprocesses, callproc.c); it draws through a toolkit and has no Vulkan driver to configure | `crates/romacs-vm-gui/src/main.rs` adds `nosam` to `RADV_PERFTEST` before anything else runs, unless the value already says `nosam` or `sam` (`romacs_renderer_wgpu::gpu_policy::radv_perftest`): Mesa's RADV then gives its command buffers system memory, not the VRAM the CPU can see. On a card with 256 MB of it and several Vulkan programs running, the kernel otherwise tries, and fails, to move those buffers into it on every command submission, 2 to 4 ms a frame on the thread that draws. Other drivers ignore the variable. `process-environment`, `initial-environment` and every subprocess see the changed value | **Visible to Lisp**: `(getenv "RADV_PERFTEST")` and a subprocess's environment have `nosam` where GNU's have what Emacs was started with | The driver reads the variable when the Vulkan instance is made, in this process, and there is no other way to ask; measured on the load-resilience rig, 4 alternating sessions each way: the cursor-only draw 0.35-0.59 ms with it, 1.6-4.5 ms without |
