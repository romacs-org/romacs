## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-RAWTEXT1 | Raw bytes (eight-bit characters #x3FFF80..#x3FFFFF) in multibyte buffers | GNU's buffer text is its internal multibyte form, where a raw byte B is the two-byte form C0/C1 80..BF; `insert`, `base64-decode-region`, `decode-coding-region`, `insert-file-contents`, process output, casing and `translate-region` put such characters in a multibyte buffer, `char-after` answers #x3FFF00+B, searches tell the raw byte from the Latin-1 character of the same code, it displays as `\NNN` in `escape-glyph`, `position-bytes` counts two bytes, `set-buffer-multibyte` converts it to its byte and back (`str_as_multibyte`) | VM2's rope is UTF-8, so a raw byte B is stored as the placeholder character U+00B (the same bytes with the lead byte two higher, so every byte position, marker, property range and character count agrees with GNU) and `TextBody::raw` (`crates/romacs-vm/src/text.rs`) lists the placeholders standing for raw bytes; every write converts through `internal_to_rope`, every Lisp-visible read (`buffer-substring`, `char-after`, searches, the regex matcher, `write-region`, undo, the reader, display elements, columns, native painting) through `rope_to_internal`/`code_at`. Pure Unicode text pays one `raw.is_empty()` test. Remaining: surrogates and characters above #x10FFFF still cannot be inserted (`invalid Unicode text`); a buffer holding raw bytes pays a sorted-offset splice per edit | **Package-observable** only for the remaining limits: `(insert #xD800)` and `(insert #x110000)` signal where GNU inserts | <!-- symbols: insert, base64-decode-region, decode-coding-region, insert-file-contents, char-after, set-buffer-multibyte, string-to-multibyte -->
