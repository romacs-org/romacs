## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-TERM-GEOMETRY | Terminal pty size under a grown mini-window | `term.el` and eat resize their pty through `window-adjust-process-window-size-function` on every window size change, so a minibuffer that grows for fido-vertical or icomplete candidates shrinks the shell's grid and restores it on exit (pinned GNU `-nw` with `term` in a 40-row tty: `stty size` 37, then 29 during `C-x C-f`, then 37) | `romacs-term`'s pty follows its controlling window's stable body: its size with a one-line mini-window, or with none while a layer hosts the minibuffer (VM2-MINI-COMPLETION-LAYER) (`window.rs` `stable_window_body`), less its bottom divider (VM2-PANE-CHROME), reconciled at every geometry publication for every terminal (`terminal_host.rs` `reconcile_terminal_geometry`). The controlling window is the selected window (the one a minibuffer was entered from, while it is selected) when it shows the terminal, else the first window showing it in tree order; a hidden terminal keeps its size. The pty changes size on a host resize, a split, a delete or a switch of controlling window, never for the mini-window. While a grown mini-window covers the grid's bottom rows, the window shows the grid from the row that keeps the cursor's row last, and from the grid's first row again once restored. `tests/terminal_host.rs`; `harness/terminal_live.py --prompt-grow` | **User-observable** (the shell sees no SIGWINCH for a prompt, as in Alacritty), and to a package only through `stty size`/`LINES` in the child | A shell redraws on every resize; following transient mini growth made the prompt jump and, with resizes driven from stale geometry, stick at the wrong row | <!-- symbols: romacs-term -->
