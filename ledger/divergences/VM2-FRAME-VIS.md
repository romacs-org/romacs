## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-FRAME-VIS | The host frame's visibility is not modelled | GNU `frame-visible-p`, `make-frame-visible`, `make-frame-invisible`, `iconify-frame`, `raise-frame` and `lower-frame` (`src/frame.c:3505-3700`) track and change a frame's visibility through its terminal; a batch terminal frame always reads visible and ignores the requests | VM2's host frame always reads visible, `visible-frame-list` lists it, the requests change nothing (so does `x-focus-frame`, which frame.el's `select-frame-set-input-focus` asks of a multi-frame display: the host's one window has the focus whenever the session does), and making the sole frame invisible without FORCE signals GNU's error. `tests/frame_visibility.rs` matches pinned GNU batch. A child frame's visibility is modelled: `make-frame-visible`, `make-frame-invisible` and a `visibility` parameter show and hide it, `frame-visible-p` and `visible-frame-list` report it, and `raise-frame` and `lower-frame` restack it among its siblings (VM2-CHILD-FRAME) | **Package-observable** only in the GUI, where iconifying or hiding the window is not performed | Model visibility with the GUI backend's window state when it owns frame lifecycle (VM2-WIN-SUBSTRATE) | <!-- symbols: frame-visible-p, make-frame-visible, make-frame-invisible, iconify-frame, raise-frame, lower-frame, x-focus-frame, visible-frame-list -->
