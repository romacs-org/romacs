## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-FRAME-VIS | A frame's visibility is modelled only where the host manages its window | GNU `frame-visible-p`, `make-frame-visible`, `make-frame-invisible`, `iconify-frame`, `raise-frame` and `lower-frame` (`src/frame.c:3505-3700`) track and change a frame's visibility through its terminal; a batch terminal frame always reads visible and ignores the requests | Where the host shows one top-level frame, that frame always reads visible, `visible-frame-list` lists it, the requests change nothing, and making the sole frame invisible without FORCE signals GNU's error; `tests/frame_visibility.rs` matches pinned GNU batch. Where the host manages its frames' windows (`HostInput::top_level_frames`), a top-level frame shows, hides and iconifies (`frame-visible-p` says `icon`), hiding needs another visible or iconified frame, and `x-focus-frame` and `raise-frame` ask the host to focus its window (`tests/top_frames.el` matches pinned GNU X11). A child frame's visibility is modelled: `make-frame-visible`, `make-frame-invisible` and a `visibility` parameter show and hide it, `frame-visible-p` and `visible-frame-list` report it, and `raise-frame` and `lower-frame` restack it among its siblings (VM2-CHILD-FRAME) | **Package-observable** only in the GUI, whose host shows one window until its display paints more (VM2-WIN-SUBSTRATE) | The GUI's host manages its windows' visibility with its second window (romacs#159) | <!-- symbols: frame-visible-p, make-frame-visible, make-frame-invisible, iconify-frame, raise-frame, lower-frame, x-focus-frame, visible-frame-list -->
