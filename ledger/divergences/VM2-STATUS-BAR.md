| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-STATUS-BAR | The root frame's status bar is the mode line | GNU draws each window's mode line in its own band, `mode-line-active` for the selected window (and for the window a minibuffer was entered from while the mini-window is selected, `CURRENT_MODE_LINE_ACTIVE_FACE_ID_3`, dispextern.h:1553), `mode-line-inactive` for the others; the root window ends at the mini-window | On a window system the root frame shows a status bar, the root frame's `romacs-status-bar-lines` parameter (`rlisp/romacs-chrome.el` sets it in every look, never in batch), between the root window and the mini-window, or with `romacs-status-bar-position` `top` (the `romacs-status-bar-position` frame parameter) under the tab bar with the root window under it, switched at once: `frame-root-window` is shorter by it while `frame-height`, `frame-text-height` and the mini-window are unchanged; its height is the row the host measures for its faces (a canonical line before one), a change lays the frame out again once, and it drops while it would leave the root window under two lines (`tests/status_bar.rs`). The bar is every root-frame window's mode line: no ordinary window of that frame draws a band (a child frame's windows keep theirs, as GNU's), so `window-mode-line-height` is 0 and each body is a line taller than GNU's, in either look (`romacs-classic-chrome` keeps the bar); a batch frame has no bar, so GNU's bands and geometry stand there and GNU's tests do not move. It shows the mode line of the window GNU's face rule treats as selected (`romacs-status-bar-window`; the window a minibuffer was entered from while the mini-window is selected; while a child frame's window is selected, the root frame's selected window, unfocused): that window's `mode-line-format` parameter or its buffer's value, in `mode-line-active`, expanded at the bar's columns with its buffer current and that window selected as GNU's display_mode_lines selects it (frame-local, restored), reading its own point and start, so text past the columns is clipped; `%-` shows nothing in the bar (`format-mode-line` still gives `--`). Unlike GNU's, no window's point is installed in its buffer: an `:eval` form reading `(point)` for another window sees its buffer's point. A nil or `none` format contributes nothing. The bar is expanded again only when GNU's redisplay would update a mode line: `force-mode-line-update` or `set-buffer-modified-p`, another window, buffer, buffer name or format, the buffer's text or modified state, the window's point, start or size, the narrowing, the minibuffer's state, faces or the bar's columns; a variable set without either call shows at the next such change, as in GNU. Only that window's line is shown: another window's own format (a buffer-local `mode-line-format`, a terminal's or a popup's, or a `mode-line-format` window parameter) shows nowhere and is never expanded, and changes in another window's buffer, a terminal's output among them, leave the bar alone. The bar is painted over that text: a 3-pixel block of the accent (`romacs-pane-focus`) at its left edge, dimmed while a child frame's window holds the focus; each `romacs-segment` run named by a `romacs-pill` face drawn on a rounded background of that face's colour, its text in its `romacs-pill-text` face's foreground, or its `romacs-pill-modified` face's while the bar's own buffer is modified; a character a `romacs-icon` alist names drawn as that icon while Romacs Icons is there, or dropped where it names `none`; segments with a `romacs-priority` dropped, lowest first, until the text fits the bar's columns less its padding; the text inside 12 pixels of padding at either end, in up to three zones: the part after a `right-align` segment flush with the right padding and whole, the part after a `centre-align` segment (and before that one) centred on the bar, at least 24 pixels from either side and pushed as little as that needs, after the left part where there is no room for both gaps, and the left part and that one clipped where the right starts, each zone a prefix of its characters (`romacs-status-bar-string` shows the same, spaces between). A mouse button released on the bar runs the binding at the clicked character of the window whose line it is (`romacs--status-bar-click`, `romacs--status-bar-target`): the `local-map` or `keymap` there in the bar's text as last painted, else the global map's, under `[mode-line BUTTON]`, with a `mode-line` position in that window whose object is that text and column; the press runs nothing (so `[mode-line down-mouse-1] mouse-drag-mode-line` never starts), wheel and drag are not delivered; the text carries the properties the mode line projects (VM2-G1B: `local-map`, `keymap`, `help-echo`), so a package reading another from `posn-string` sees nil; a marker's room and the room past the line run nothing; while a prompt reads input the GUI host delivers no pointer event to Lisp. Layers, the completion panel and toasts keep clear of the bar: above one at the bottom (`romacs-chrome-bottom`), below one at the top (`romacs-chrome-top`), restacked when it moves.
