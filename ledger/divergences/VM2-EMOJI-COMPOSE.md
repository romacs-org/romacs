## Font selection order

| # | Scope | GNU | VM2 | Reason / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-EMOJI-COMPOSE | Emoji and variation sequences on a window-system frame: ZWJ sequences, VS-16 (`❤️`), skin tones, tag sequences, flags | GNU composes them through `composition-function-table` (`composite.el` for VS-16 and the other variation selectors, `emoji-zwj.el` for the exact ZWJ, flag and tag sequences Unicode lists) under `auto-composition-mode`, shaping each with its font; on a graphical frame VS-16 makes `font_range` choose the emoji font, which `fontset-default` gives the `emoji` script ("Noto Color Emoji", then "Noto Emoji", `fontset.el`). | The evaluator keeps a sequence's joiners as characters instead of glyphless glyphs and the host lays the sequence out as one glyph at its first character, as wide as its representative character's glyph, the rest taking no room, point inside it showing on it (`romacs-layout-input` `cluster`, `romacs-layout`); a base and its selectors show that base's glyph from the representative's font (the emoji font for VS-16), a ZWJ sequence as the renderer shapes it. The sequences are Unicode's rules (an emoji ZWJ an emoji, a selector after a non-ASCII base, a skin tone or tags after an emoji, two regional indicators) rather than `composition-function-table` and `auto-composition-mode`, which are not consulted; combining marks and script shaping are not composed. Of `fontset-default` the host applies only the `emoji` script's fonts (`romacs-font` `fontset_default`). | Emoji in mail, chat and agent transcripts show as GNU shows them; GNU's table is the same rule for the sequences fonts have. Pinned by `crates/romacs-layout/tests/emoji.rs` and `crates/romacs-vm-gui/harness/emoji_live.py` (crops against pinned GNU X11). |
