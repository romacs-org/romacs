## Macro redefinition and the code that expanded it

| # | Mechanism | GNU | Romacs | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-MACRO-REDEFINE | What a compiled function records of the macros it expanded, and what a later write to one does to it | `load` expands each top-level form whole before evaluating it (`src/lread.c:2133-2150`, `lisp/emacs-lisp/macroexp.el:915`), and the compiler expands at compile time; `fset`/`defalias` set only the cell (`src/data.c:897-935`), so callers keep the old expansion. Nothing records which code expanded which macro. | Slice S1 (redesign macro-invalidation): each compiled function records the `(slot, version)` of every macro cell, alias link and compiler-macro handler it expanded through (`crates/romacs-vm/src/macro_record.rs`), `romacs-who-macroexpands` answers who expanded a cell, and `romacs-macro-invalidation-stats` lists the writes to recorded cells with what each would make stale. A redefinition still leaves callers on the old expansion, as GNU does; S2 makes them re-expand at their next call. Cost: `--loadup` (the loadup chain from source) runs +27.9 M instructions:u, +0.170% (median of 6 alternating runs against base `0f3846da1`, pairs +18 M to +39 M), the world build alone, as a restored session maps the records with its image. The loadup chain's own double loads (`macroexp.el`, `cl-macs.el`, `help.el`) are 14 such writes reaching 34 functions. | **Not observable yet**: S1 adds the record, two query primitives and `romacs--record-macroexpansion`, which `macroexpand-1` and `macroexp--compiler-macro` call; no evaluation changes. The cost is accepted: an allocation-free record store would be a mechanism of its own to save about 10 ms of world build. | <!-- symbols: defmacro, macroexpand, macroexpand-1, fset, defalias, romacs-who-macroexpands, romacs-macro-invalidation-stats -->
