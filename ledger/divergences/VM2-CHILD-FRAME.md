## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-CHILD-FRAME | Child frames (`make-frame` with `parent-frame`) | A window system's `x-create-frame` (pgtkfns.c:1197) makes top-level and child frames; a child frame is a window-system window inside its parent's, with its own minibuffer unless told otherwise, fringes, scroll bars and tool bars as its parameters say, placed and stacked by the toolkit (`z-group` above or below its siblings) relative to its parent's native origin, receiving pointer events; `delete-frame` runs `delete-frame-functions` for each descendant it deletes; a child frame's text area is at least `window-min-height` lines unless its `min-height` parameter is a number; X applies size requests asynchronously | Every frame `make-frame` makes is a child frame (`romacs--create-frame`, reached through romacs-pgtk.el's `frame-creation-function` method; without a `parent-frame` it signals), painted by the host as a separate layer over the root frame in creation order, placed from the root frame's native origin, outside its internal border (VM2-PANE-CHROME), as GNU places it (raise/lower restack siblings; `z-group` is stored, not read), with its text inside its `child-frame-border-width` (else `internal-border-width`) border on its `background-color` (a `#RRGGBB` parameter) or the default face's background. It shares its parent's minibuffer window, which one child frame may host and draw (`romacs--host-minibuffer`, VM2-MINI-COMPLETION-LAYER), and has no scroll bars, menu, tool or tab bar of its own (its fringes are its `left-fringe` and `right-fringe` parameters'); its size is in the session's character cell, at least one line of two columns; position parameters take integers (negative from the far edge), `(+ N)`, `(- N)` and fractions. The queries packages observe match pinned GNU X11 (`frame-list` newest first, `visible-frame-list`, `frame-parent`, `frame-ancestor-p`, `frame-root-window`, `frame-selected-window`, `selected-window` per frame, `select-frame`/`select-window` across frames, `window-frame`, `window-list`, `next-window`/`previous-window`, `get-buffer-window` ALL-FRAMES, `frame-parameter(s)`, `frame-position`, `set-frame-position`, `set-frame-size`, `modify-frame-parameters` geometry and visibility, `delete-frame` and its hooks, `redirect-frame-focus`): `crates/romacs-vm/tests/child_frames.rs` against `child_frames.gnu` (pinned GNU X11 on Xvfb, `child_frames_oracle.py`). Showing, moving, resizing or hiding a child frame never resizes a window of its parent or a terminal; window configurations are the host frame's. A child frame's cursor is drawn only while one of its windows is selected. A child frame shows its parent's faces, what `x-create-frame-with-faces` computes for a frame on the same display, so `copy-face` and `face-attribute` work on it; the bare primitive therefore answers for named faces where GNU's bare `x-create-frame` has only `default` (`tests/child_frames.rs` `a_child_frame_has_its_parents_faces`). It shares them, rather than copying them, until a face change on it alone would make it differ (`internal-set-lisp-face-attribute`, `internal-make-lisp-face` or `internal-copy-lisp-face` with the frame); then it gets its own copy, as GNU's frame has from the start. While it shares, `face-spec-recalc` and `internal-merge-in-global-face` there do nothing (VM2-FACE-ENGINE), so a theme switch keeps it sharing, and a new-frame default its parent has since overridden shows as its parent's; a change for all frames reaches it through its parent, `frame--face-hash-table` is the parent's, and its `foreground-color` and `background-color` parameters, unless set, are the parent's (`romacs--frame-shares-faces-p`; `a_child_frame_shares_its_parents_faces_until_one_is_set_on_it`). A theme or a `defface` therefore recomputes faces on one frame however many layers are open; GNU recomputes them on every frame, child frames included. The display realizes every frame's faces from the root frame's lface vectors: an edit on a child frame alone does not reach it. A child frame's `background-color` colours only its display while it shares: unlike GNU's (xfaces.c `update_face_from_frame_parameter`), setting it leaves the shared default face, so a glass layer keeps sharing its parent's faces; a sharing frame also holds no `background-mode`, `display-type`, `cursor-color` or `border-color` of its own, and takes the parent's `(supports ...)` answers. On a frame with faces of its own, as in GNU, a new `foreground-color` or `background-color` becomes the default face's, and a new default background recomputes the background mode (`frame-set-background-mode`); a child frame getting its own faces takes its parent's `background-mode` and `display-type` with them. Text with an unspecified background on a child frame shows its `background-color` (frame.c `gui_set_background_color`); `face-remapping-alist` is not applied to any frame yet. Romacs's own parameters `romacs-layer-radius`, `romacs-layer-glow` and `romacs-layer-shadow` round a layer's corners (leaving its border as unpainted padding) and light it (VM2-ECHO-LAYER, VM2-MINI-COMPLETION-LAYER). Pointer events over a child frame are not delivered to it (the GUI host delivers no pointer events to Lisp yet), descendants are deleted without their own `delete-frame-functions`, and `window-size-change-functions` and `window-configuration-change-hook` do not run for a child frame (Romacs runs `window-size-change-functions` from redisplay when a window's size changed, as GNU does: `lisp/romacs/vm/window-change.el`) | **Package-observable:** posframe-style and keymap-popup's child-frame code runs and paints (`harness/popup_live.py --child-frame`, `--which-key` through `romacs-layer.el`), but a top-level frame cannot be made, a popup can be smaller than GNU allows, and it cannot be clicked | A layer over the frame that never resizes the windows or terminals below it is the UI direction; one top-level frame is what the host displays | <!-- symbols: make-frame, romacs--create-frame, romacs--frame-shares-faces-p, face-spec-recalc, delete-frame, frame-parent, frame-ancestor-p, frame-position, set-frame-position, set-frame-size, set-frame-width, set-frame-height, frame-text-width, frame-text-height, frame-text-cols, frame-text-lines, frame-total-cols, frame-total-lines, redirect-frame-focus, frame-focus, frame--set-was-invisible, frame-after-make-frame, set-window-fringes, set-window-scroll-bars, select-frame, visible-frame-list, raise-frame, lower-frame -->
