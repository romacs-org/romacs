## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LISP-TABULATED-LIST | What a `tabulated-list-mode` list is | `tabulated-list-mode` derives from `special-mode`; `tabulated-list-print-entry` gives each entry's line the properties `tabulated-list-id` and `tabulated-list-entry` (`lisp/emacs-lisp/tabulated-list.el`). | `tabulated-list-mode` derives from `romacs-list-mode` (`lisp/ui/romacs-list.el`, loaded by `lisp/loadup.el` just before it). `tabulated-list-print-entry` also gives the line `romacs-list-row` `(:id ID :kind entry)`, so each entry is a row of the list (`romacs-list-row`, `-shown-rows`, `-next`, `-goto`); a mark over a row's first columns leaves it a row. Every tabulated list highlights its row (`hl-line-mode` in face `romacs-list-row`, the theme's `hl-line` background), and `tabulated-list-print` moves the highlight to where it leaves point. Printing, UPDATE, REMEMBER-POS, groups and the fake header are GNU's, unchanged. A list printed by a printer of its own has no rows. A banner (`romacs-list-banners`) is not drawn on a tabulated list: its packages read its lines from `point-min` as entries. `test/romacs/lisp/romacs-list-tabulated-tests.el` | **Package-observable:** `derived-mode-p 'romacs-list-mode` holds for every tabulated list; `hl-line-mode` is on in it, with its overlay; entries' lines carry one more text property. Buffer text, ids, entries, tags, point and UPDATE are GNU's; GNU's tabulated-list, package, buff-menu, dired, shell, keymap and simple tests and the bundled gnosis and jabber suites give the same outcomes (gnosis's dashboard snapshot leaves out hl-line's overlay, which follows point after each command). One list mechanism: tabulated lists are Romacs lists, with the list's row highlight and row API. | <!-- symbols: tabulated-list-mode, tabulated-list-print, tabulated-list-print-entry, romacs-list-mode, romacs-list-row, hl-line-mode -->
