| LISP-TABULATED-LIST | How `tabulated-list-mode` lists draw | `tabulated-list-mode` derives from `special-mode`; `tabulated-list-print` erases the buffer and inserts each entry with `tabulated-list-printer`, or with UPDATE walks the lines in sort order, keeping those whose id is shown and deleting the rest (`lisp/emacs-lisp/tabulated-list.el`). | `tabulated-list-mode` derives from `romacs-list-mode` (`lisp/ui/romacs-list.el`, loaded by `lisp/loadup.el` just before it), so every tabulated list highlights its row (`hl-line-mode`, face `romacs-list-row`) and can show a banner by its mode in `romacs-list-banners` (none by default).
