## Observable by a package

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-TREESIT-SHOW-FIRST | When a tree-sitter buffer's edit is parsed, against the frame that shows it | Redisplay runs `pre-redisplay-functions`, whose `treesit--pre-redisplay` forces the parse, and fontifies before the frame shows; a typed character waits for its parse (`treesit.el`, `xdisp.c`). | A frame after an edit of `romacs-treesit-show-before-parse` bytes or fewer (default 8; zero waits as GNU) skips `pre-redisplay-functions` and the windows' fontification: the character shows with the faces it inherited, and the next frame, which always parses and fontifies, follows as soon as the first is drawn (`Vm::defer_parse`, `lisp/romacs/romacs-treesit.el`). A larger edit, such as a yank, waits for its parse. A visit's first parse runs on a thread from the moment its text is read; until it lands, `treesit--pre-redisplay` does not run and the window shows the text plain, and the frame its landing wakes runs the hook with every change since. `tests/treesit.rs` | **User-observable** only: for one frame a typed character carries the face it inherited, and a region overlay or a hook's change lags it; a large file's text shows before its faces. Lisp sees the same order of events. A deliberate default: a parse of a large function takes longer than a frame. | <!-- symbols: romacs-treesit-show-before-parse -->
