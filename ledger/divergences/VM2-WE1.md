| VM2-WE1 | `window-end` with UPDATE in a displayed window | GNU `Fwindow_end` (`src/window.c:1782`) returns Z less the recorded `window_end_pos`, unless UPDATE is non-nil, the end may be stale (`windows_or_buffers_changed || !w->window_end_valid || b->clip_changed || window_outdated (w)`) and the session is not batch: then it runs `start_display` at the clipped window start, `move_it_vertically` by the text area's height and `move_it_past_eol` when that row starts inside the window, and returns the iterator's position | Batch, and any window before a host has applied a layout, answers the recorded end with or without UPDATE, as GNU's noninteractive session does (`tests/window_end.rs`, against pinned GNU).
