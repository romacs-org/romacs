The message of an error the command loop reports, or of an undefined key, leads with the error glyph and is outlined (a 1 px hairline, `romacs-layer-outline-face`) and glows (`romacs-layer-glow-face`) in `romacs-layer-toast-error`'s colour, its text in the toast's own; a toast's text is wrapped at words into the rows its window shows, tabs at the toast's own stops (8). Where the minibuffer's layer shows over the toasts' corner, they start below it. A value inserted (a prefix argument) or shown in *Pp Eval Output* has no chip.
