## Buffer lifecycle remaining boundaries

| # | mechanism | GNU | romacs | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VM2-BUFLIFE2 | the buffer that replaces a killed one | Fkill_buffer replaces the buffer in windows, makes `other-buffer`'s choice current, and orders candidates by the frame's buffer list | As Fkill_buffer, `lisp/vm/buffer_lifecycle.el` calls `replace-buffer-in-windows` once window.el defines it, makes `other-buffer`'s choice current in place of a killed current buffer, and keeps the buffer when it is its own choice (a sole `*scratch*`) or the minibuffer's only fallback. Where that leaves the buffer shown or current (the bare VM, or `other-buffer` finding none), the native kill takes other_buffer_safely's choice: the first live buffer not beginning with a space and not the minibuffer's, else a new `*scratch*`. Romacs keeps no frame buffer list (VM2-FRAME1), so `other-buffer` scans the buffer list alone and `buffer-list` of a live frame is the buffer list. `other-buffer` reads that order once, at entry: a frame `buffer-predicate` that reorders buffers mid-scan (burying one) does not change what the call visits, where GNU's walk of its live list would | Observable only where GNU's frame buffer list would order the candidates differently, or where a frame predicate reorders the buffer list | <!-- symbols: kill-buffer, other-buffer, buffer-list -->
